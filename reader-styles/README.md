# Reader styles

The [standard](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.0.0/standard.md) and the [principles](../principles/README.md) assume a general audience. A context says what moves when the audience is known. Each lists only overrides; everything else holds.

| File | The context |
|---|---|
| [academic-audience.md](academic-audience.md) | conference and seminar talks, and the defence of a thesis or an internship report |
| [business-decision-maker.md](business-decision-maker.md) | a pitch, a sales presentation or a decision deck, for someone who will decide |
| [casual-audience.md](casual-audience.md) | a meetup, a club, a lightning talk or friends: a room that chose to come and can leave |

The format is the [template](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.0.0/reader-styles/template.md) of reader-ergonomics.
