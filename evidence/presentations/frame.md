# Frame: the problem and its names

This file states the problem in one sentence and lists what research and practice call its parts.

## The method has a name

This review is a **multivocal literature review (MLR)**, after Garousi 2019: a systematic review that adds **grey literature** to the formal literature. Grey literature is any source outside journals and books from academic presses. Grey sources come in three **tiers** by how much control the outlet exerts:

- Tier 1: books, standards, government and official reports, white papers.
- Tier 2: owner web guides, annual reports, presentations, videos, wiki pages.
- Tier 3: blogs, emails, social posts.

How the pool was built is in [protocol](protocol.md); each source and its score are in [sources](sources.md).

## The problem in one sentence

So that a listener understands and keeps the main points, decide what the slides show and what the speaker says; the listener cannot slow down or reread.

## What is new against a page

| Property | Page | Talk |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | the reader sets it | the speaker sets it (**system-paced**) |
| Reread | possible | not possible |
| Source of words | one channel | slide text and voice at once |
| Speaker | absent | part of the medium |
| Seeing the words | at reading distance | at room distance |

## Names for the same things

- Learning theory: **multimedia learning**, **cognitive theory of multimedia learning (CTML)**, **cognitive load theory (CLT)**.
- Principles with a name each: **modality**, **redundancy**, **segmenting**, **signalling**, **coherence**, **spatial contiguity**, **embodiment**, **voice**, **image**.
- Slide design: **assertion-evidence (AE)**, **sentence headline**, **action title** (consulting), **topic-subtopic** or **phrase headline** slide, **bullet slide**, **slideware**, **presentation graphics**.
- Speaker: **instructor presence**, **social agency theory**, **pedagogical agent**, **gesture**, **enthusiasm**.
- Genre: **conference presentation (CP)**, **scientific conference presentation**, **research talk**, **move analysis**, **multimodal genre**, **Q&A** or **discussion session**.
- Room: **legibility**, **viewing distance**, **display image size**, **element height**, **ICT accessibility**.

## Motion in a talk (Q7): names

- Problem in one sentence: decide what moves, who sets its pace, and what is shown live rather than recorded, when the speaker and not the viewer controls time.
- Research on animation: **instructional animation**, **dynamic visualization**, **learner control** or **interactivity principle**, **system-paced** against **self-paced**, **transient information**.
- Research on slides and video: **cumulative** or **sequential presentation**, **temporal contiguity**, **segmenting**, **seductive details**, **lecture capture**, **video lecture**, **instructor presence**, **guided gaze**.
- Research on demonstration: **live coding** against **static-code examples**, **demonstration**, **explainer video**, **whiteboard animation**.
- Practice: **builds** or **build steps**, **progressive disclosure**, **reveal**, **fly-in**, **transition**, **Morph**, **embedded clip**, **live demo**, **staged** or **live-looking demo**, **screencast**, **talking head**, **Khan-style** tablet video, **3Blue1Brown style**, **Manim** (the library behind it).
- Acronyms and authors tried: CTML, CLT, MOOC, ALC and TLC; Guo, Brame, Tabbers, Liu, Shah, Selvaraj, Sanderson.
- Animation against static pictures in a self-paced page is held in the [learning science review](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.12.0/evidence/learning-science/review.md) (animation section) and is not reviewed again here.

## Colour (Q8): names

- Problem in one sentence: decide which colours go on slides and figures so that they signal, stay readable in a room and for colour-vision deficiency, and do not mislead.
- Research on learning: **color coding** or **signaling** (cueing), **emotional design** (**pleasant**, **warm**, **aesthetic** colours), **anthropomorphism**.
- Research on figures: **color scale** or **colour map**, **perceptually uniform**, **sequential**, **diverging** and **qualitative** schemes, **rainbow** or **jet** map.
- Research on viewers: **color-vision deficiency (CVD)**, **color blindness**, **positive** and **negative polarity**, **color harmony**, **color preference**, **ecological valence theory**.
- Practice: **palette**, **color scheme**, **accent colour**, **color wheel** (**complementary**, **analogous**, **triadic**), **dark mode**, **inverse video**, **colour-safe** or **colourblind-friendly** palette, **Okabe-Ito** or **Wong** palette, **viridis**, **#EndRainbow**, **ColorBrewer**.
- Acronyms and authors tried: CVD, WCAG 1.4.1, CIELAB; Ozcelik, Plass, Brom, Wong, Harrower, Brewer, Borland, Crameri, Okabe, Greco, Palmer, Schloss.
- Contrast ratio and positive polarity are held in [sufficient contrast](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.12.0/principles/page/sufficient-contrast.md); position and length before colour is held in [draw relations](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.12.0/principles/page/draw-relations.md); the colour-by-kind preference is in the [visual-relational reader style](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/v1.12.0/reader-styles/visual-relational.md). None is reviewed again.
