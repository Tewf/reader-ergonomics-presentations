# Figures: how to draw a figure or a diagram for a reader

Question: how should a figure or a diagram be drawn, in any document of the family, so that a reader understands its message quickly and correctly, can reach it without sight, and trusts it.

Scope: any drawn picture in a document: a chart, a node-link diagram, a schematic ([decision 0006](../../decisions/0006-the-visual-standard-of-the-family.md)). Colour and motion on slides stay in the [presentations](../presentations/README.md) topic (strands 7 and 8). The theory of diagram notations and the layout studies of software diagrams are in reader-ergonomics-software-design's [system diagrams review](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-software-design/tree/master/evidence/system-diagrams), linked, not reviewed again.

Steps: [frame](frame.md) · [protocol](protocol.md) · [sources](sources.md) · [review](review.md) · [state of the art](state-of-the-art.md) · [position](position.md) · [against](against.md) · [baseline](baseline.md) · [bibliography](../bibliography.md)

## Claims

| Claim | Grade | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Of five layout aesthetics, fewer edge crossings had the largest effect on understanding a node-link diagram | measured | review.md 1 |
| Crossings that remain cost least near 90 degrees | supported with caveat (small samples) | review.md 1 |
| One study found layouts with crossings fastest to interpret, while readers preferred none | supported with caveat (26 participants) | review.md 1, against.md |
| For quantities, position on a common scale is read more accurately than length, angle or area | measured, for charts | review.md 2 |
| Items inside one bounded region are seen as a group, which can override proximity | measured, in the laboratory | review.md 2 |
| Beauty gives a small gain in performance: g = 0.12 over 25 studies, g = 0.29 over 31 | measured, for interfaces | review.md 3 |
| Embellished charts were described as accurately as plain ones and remembered better weeks later; other embellishments slowed search or lowered accuracy | measured, disputed | review.md 3, against.md |
| Text art is non-text content: it needs a text alternative like any image | convention (a standard) | review.md 4 |
| A complex diagram needs a short and a long text alternative; a filename is a failure | convention (a standard) | review.md 4 |
| Lines and fills that carry meaning need 3:1 contrast against what lies next to them | convention (a standard) | review.md 4 |
| No study tests text art against a drawn diagram, with readers or with screen-reader users | not found | review.md 4 |
| Style guides agree on a stated message, direct labels and little decoration, without studies behind them | convention, untested | review.md 5 |
