# Position for quantities

**Claim:** a figure that shows a quantity shows it by position on a common scale, or by length, before angle or area.

- **Grade:** measured, for charts.
- **Evidence:** [review, strand 2](../../evidence/figures/review.md).
- **Check:** `figure-judgment` (judgment): is a quantity shown by area or angle where position would do?
- **Reader styles:** none changes it.

## What it asks

- Prefer a dot or a bar on a common axis to a pie, a bubble or a 3D mark.
- Align bars on one baseline, side by side.

## Why

Readers judge position on a common scale most accurately (Cleveland 1984). A crowdsourced replication kept the ranking: position beat length, and area did worse than angle (Heer 2010). Aligned and adjacent bars beat stacked and separated ones (Talbot 2014).

## Limits

- Angle was not worse than length in the replication (Heer 2010).
- The evidence is for charts; a diagram's boxes and links carry no quantity.
