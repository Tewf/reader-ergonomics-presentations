# Group by region

**Claim:** what belongs together is shown by a bounded region or a joining line, with colour as a second cue and never the only one.

- **Grade:** supported with caveat (laboratory evidence).
- **Evidence:** [review, strand 2](../../evidence/figures/review.md).
- **Check:** `figure-judgment` (judgment): is membership shown by colour alone anywhere?
- **Reader styles:** none changes it.

## What it asks

- Draw a group as a box around its members, or join its members by a line.
- Colour follows [colour as a signal](../slide/colour-as-a-signal.md): one colour per kind of thing, never the only cue.

## Why

Items inside one bounded region are seen as a group, which can override proximity and similarity (Palmer 1992). Colour must never be the only cue (W3C 2023c).

## Limits

- The evidence is from laboratory displays, not diagrams. Grouping by a joining line (Palmer and Rock, 1994) was not read.
