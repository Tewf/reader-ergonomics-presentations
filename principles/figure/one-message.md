# One message

**Claim:** a figure has one message, and its title or caption states it.

- **Grade:** supported with caveat.
- **Evidence:** [review, strands 2 and 5](../../evidence/figures/review.md).
- **Check:** `figure-judgment` (judgment): does the caption state what the reader should take from the figure?
- **Reader styles:** none changes it.

## What it asks

- Write the caption as a sentence that says what the figure shows, not a label of its type.
- Split a figure that carries two messages.

## Why

Eye movements and descriptions of 393 visualisations led to the advice that titles and supporting text should convey the message (Borkin 2016). Practice says the same: fix the message before drawing (Rougier 2014); write a concise, active title (Urban 2025).

## Limits

- No study compares a stated message with a label title on a diagram.
