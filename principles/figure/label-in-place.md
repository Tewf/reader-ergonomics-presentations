# Label in place

**Claim:** each node and link is labelled where it sits; a legend is used only when direct labels do not fit.

- **Grade:** supported with caveat; for direct labels, mainly convention.
- **Evidence:** [review, strands 2 and 5](../../evidence/figures/review.md).
- **Check:** `figure-judgment` (judgment): is any part named only through a legend that labels could replace?
- **Reader styles:** none changes it.

## What it asks

- Write the label on the node, and the label of a link beside it, near its middle.
- When a legend is needed, put it close to the data, in the order the data appears.

## Why

In bar and line graphs, a legend placed compatibly with the data shortened the time to understand a graph and to recall it (Huestegge 2011). Style guides ask for direct labels and no legend when possible (Urban 2025; Datawrapper 2022, example only).

## Limits

- Huestegge 2011 tested where a legend sits, not direct labels against a legend.
- No study of labels in node-link diagrams was found.
